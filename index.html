Put it on your Home Screen

PDF Ninja runs in Safari. Adding it to the Home Screen gives it an icon that opens the editor full screen, like any other app.

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Files you open come from the Files app, iCloud Drive, or any app that shares a document to Safari. Saved files go through the share sheet to Files, Mail, or another app.
